# Golden Apple III.1 Construction Pass

## Apple silhouette

The crown now has a central apple indentation with fuller lobes and broader
cheeks. The upper body is wider and the lower body tapers back toward the
outfit.

## Ledger anchor

The Grocer ledger is moved closer to the left hand and rotated five degrees.
Equipment paints before the hand so the hand visually grips the book.

## Blink

Closed eyelids are a dedicated face-overlay illustration painted above the
open eyes.
