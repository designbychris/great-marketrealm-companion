# Approved Apple Silhouette

The Apple Fructan benchmark uses a broad, round fruit silhouette.

Key construction rules:

- shallow indentation at the crown;
- full left and right apple lobes;
- broad cheeks;
- lower fruit taper into the Grocer outfit;
- compact leaf cap rather than a horizontal leafy headdress;
- preserve oversized expressive eyes and soft storybook proportions.

This is the preferred shape benchmark for future Apple Fructan variants.
