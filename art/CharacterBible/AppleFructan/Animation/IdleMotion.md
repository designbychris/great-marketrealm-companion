# Apple Fructan Idle Motion

## Blink

Random interval of four to nine seconds, with an occasional natural double
blink.

## Breathing

One-and-a-half pixel vertical rise and a 0.6% height change over 5.6 seconds.

## Leaves

A slow sway between approximately -0.8 and +1 degree.

## Satchel

A delayed movement of less than one pixel and 0.75 degree.

## Pollen

A four-pixel upward drift over 7.5 seconds.

The static portrait remains the canonical artwork. Animation is decorative.
