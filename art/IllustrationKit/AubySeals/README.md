# Auby Seal Illustration Kit

The primary reference is `seal-of-approval.svg`.

## Rules

- Keep Auby recognisable as an aubergine.
- Primary ink is Auby Purple.
- Circular lines should contain slight irregularity.
- Small gaps and ink distress are desirable.
- Do not make the stamp geometrically sterile.
- Keep the central Auby portrait readable at small sizes.
- Gold is reserved for rare or prestigious states.
