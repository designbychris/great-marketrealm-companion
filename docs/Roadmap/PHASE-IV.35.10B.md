# Phase IV.35.10B — The Boss Would Like to Participate

The Companion-to-Tabletop Bestiary bridge now distinguishes between Companion
encounter readiness and Tabletop deployment readiness.

A canonical or published Steward creature may be projected to Tabletop when it
has a concrete Armor Class and Hit Points. A missing Dexterity score continues
to prevent Companion encounter initiative from being inferred, but no longer
hides an otherwise deployable creature from the Tabletop Keeper's Bestiary.

Publication status remains respected. Reference-only creatures lacking AC/HP
are still not exported, and no statistics are invented.
