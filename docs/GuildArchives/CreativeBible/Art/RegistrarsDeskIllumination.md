# The Registrar's Desk Illumination

## Purpose

The Registrar's Desk is the ceremonial final stage of character creation.
Auby's note introduces the whole desk, the Guild portrait is illuminated,
and the provisional Guild Record is written alongside it.

## Sequence

1. The desk enters the viewport.
2. Auby's full-width note is already waiting.
3. The portrait background washes in.
4. The adventurer is painted from body to expression.
5. Clothing and equipment settle into place.
6. Golden pollen appears in foreground and background depth.
7. The woodland frame illuminates.
8. Auby's aubergine seal is stamped last.
9. The living portrait begins breathing and blinking.

## Accessibility

Reduced-motion visitors see the complete static portrait immediately.
No required information depends on animation.

## Painted benchmark

The Golden Apple II pass adds:

- richer eye depth and secondary catchlights;
- broader asymmetrical leaves;
- apron folds, pocket and repaired stitch;
- worn satchel edges and a crooked flap;
- brush marks and gold-ink imperfections in the frame.
