# Purple Thumbprint — Approved Seal and Apple

## Seal

The lower `APPROVAL` arc has been moved down by six SVG units.

This preserves the same curvature and tracking while creating visual clearance
between the lettering and Auby's central portrait.

## Apple Fructan

The benchmark Apple now uses a single clean fruit outline.

The earlier artwork contained a repeated body contour after the fruit outline,
and the shadow layer included a lower ellipse. Together these produced a
visible secondary line around the chin.

The approved construction now uses:

- one closed round Apple silhouette;
- separate arms;
- fruit-only right-side shadow;
- no lower chin ellipse;
- no lower highlight contour;
- unchanged living-eye, leaf, outfit and equipment layers.
