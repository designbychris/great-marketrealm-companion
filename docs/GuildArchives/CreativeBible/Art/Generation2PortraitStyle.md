# The Illuminated Generation 2 Portrait Style

## Visual target

Generation 2 portraits should share Auby's warmth and dimensionality:

- rounded silhouettes;
- large glossy eyes;
- coloured outlines rather than black;
- soft top-left lighting;
- layered local-colour shadows;
- broad highlights and small specular accents;
- subtle cheek colour;
- well-loved clothing and equipment;
- restrained storybook texture.

## Construction

Each major material should include:

```text
base colour
shadow shape
highlight shape
small detail accents
local-colour outline
```

## Proportions

- Head and face occupy roughly 40–48% of the visible character height.
- Hands are slightly oversized for expressive posing.
- Feet are broad and grounded.
- Eyes sit low enough to leave room for heritage shapes, leaves or caps.
- Equipment should read clearly at thumbnail size.

## Lighting

The key light always comes from the upper left. Shadows fall towards the lower
right. This rule allows independent body and outfit layers to feel painted
together.

## The Auby test

A portrait passes the style test when it feels plausible that Auby belongs in
the same illustration without either character looking out of place.
