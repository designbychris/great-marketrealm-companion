# True Painted Blink

Golden Apple III.3 completes the living portrait blink.

During the blink:

1. the open-eye layer receives `is-blinking`;
2. the open-eye artwork becomes transparent;
3. the painted closed-eyelid overlay becomes visible;
4. after roughly 125 milliseconds, the two layers swap back.

This avoids seeing the open eyes through or behind the closed eyelids and
preserves the hand-painted expression.
