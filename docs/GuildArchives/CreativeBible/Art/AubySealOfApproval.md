# Auby Seal of Approval

## Meaning

The Auby Seal of Approval is the personal Guild mark of Auby, Master
Illuminator and Keeper of the Guild Archives.

It signifies that a portrait, record, certificate or achievement has been:

- seen;
- recorded;
- cared for;
- approved by the Guild Illuminator.

## Primary treatment

The standard seal uses Auby Purple with a one-colour waving portrait of Auby
inside a distressed circular ink mark.

The words read:

```text
SEAL OF
APPROVAL
```

No two physical impressions are assumed to be exactly identical.

## Animation

The digital stamp follows four beats:

1. **Appear** — the stamp descends toward the parchment.
2. **Impact** — it squashes against the surface and an ink splatter flashes.
3. **Settle** — it rebounds by a few pixels.
4. **Dry** — the ink loses a little saturation and remains permanently.

## Variants

- Ink — primary.
- One Colour — utility.
- Embossed — formal Guild documents.
- Gold — rare honours.

## Usage

Approved placements include:

- Guild Illuminator portraits;
- Auby Quotes;
- Guild Honours;
- certificates;
- letters from the Guild;
- important completed records.
