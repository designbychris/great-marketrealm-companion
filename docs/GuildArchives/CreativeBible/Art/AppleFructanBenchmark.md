# Apple Fructan Benchmark

The Apple Fructan Grocer is the locked Generation 2 quality reference.

## It establishes

- compact Fructan proportions;
- face and expression placement;
- leaf and stem construction;
- local-colour outlines;
- layered fruit, leaf, fabric and leather rendering;
- standard hand, boot and equipment anchors;
- the minimum detail level for future illuminated portraits.

## Auby's mark

Generated portraits will eventually carry a small aubergine-shaped Illuminator's
seal to show that Auby recorded and painted the Guild portrait.
