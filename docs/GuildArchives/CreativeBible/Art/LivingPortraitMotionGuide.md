# Living Portrait Motion Guide

## Purpose

Generation 2 portraits may move subtly because they are enchanted Guild
illuminations. Motion should suggest life without becoming a cartoon loop.

## Approved idle motion

1. Natural blink every four to nine seconds.
2. Very slight breathing.
3. Gentle leaf and stem sway.
4. Slow golden-pollen drift.
5. Delayed satchel settling.
6. Occasional glint on Auby's Illuminator mark.

## Limits

- Character movement remains below two pixels.
- Rotation remains near one degree.
- Blinks last roughly one eighth of a second.
- Motion pauses when the document is hidden.
- Motion never communicates required information.
- All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Auby's mark

The small aubergine seal identifies generated Guild portraits as illuminated
and recorded by Auby, Keeper of the Guild Archives.
