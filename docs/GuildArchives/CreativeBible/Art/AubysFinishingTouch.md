# Auby's Finishing Touch

## Painted eyelids

Living portraits blink using a dedicated closed-eyelid illustration rather
than by compressing the open-eye SVG layer.

This is more reliable across browsers and gives the blink a genuinely
painted appearance.

## The final flourish

During the last stage of illumination, Auby briefly enters the portrait
with his brush, adds a touch of golden paint, retreats, and then leaves his
aubergine seal.

The sequence reinforces that portraits are illuminated by a character in
the world rather than generated by an interface.

## Reduced motion

Auby's movement is omitted entirely when reduced motion is requested. The
completed portrait and seal appear immediately.
