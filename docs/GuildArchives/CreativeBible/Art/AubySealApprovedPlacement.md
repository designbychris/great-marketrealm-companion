# Auby Seal — Approved Placement

## Canonical mark

The Auby Seal of Approval uses circular typography.

`SEAL OF` follows the upper arc and `APPROVAL` follows the lower arc.
The centre contains a one-colour waving Auby illustration.

## Auby Quotes

The seal replaces the earlier circular Auby portrait medallion.

Placement:

- top-left;
- one seal per note;
- Auby's written signature remains;
- the quill remains as the personal handwritten finishing touch.

## Guild Illuminator

The seal is stamped inside the portrait canvas at the bottom-left.

It replaces the earlier small purple circular Guild Illuminator ornament.
The temporary Auby finishing-touch animation remains separate and may still
appear before the seal lands.

## Colour hierarchy

- Purple ink: normal approval.
- Embossed parchment: formal records.
- Gold foil: rare honours and prestigious milestones.
