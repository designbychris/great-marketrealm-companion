# Auby Sticky Notes

Auby Sticky Notes are small torn pieces of parchment held in place with purple
tape.

They are used for personal, contextual messages rather than formal Guild
records.

## Visual rules

- torn irregular edges;
- warm parchment;
- purple tape;
- handwritten body copy;
- small Auby doodle;
- slight rotation;
- never perfectly aligned.

## Tone

Notes can be:

- helpful;
- funny;
- observant;
- encouraging;
- occasionally reflective.

They should never interrupt the player with modal behaviour.

## Future states

Later phases may allow notes to be:

- folded;
- collected in Auby's Correspondence;
- discovered in the Leather Satchel;
- attached to achievements;
- left after a period away.
