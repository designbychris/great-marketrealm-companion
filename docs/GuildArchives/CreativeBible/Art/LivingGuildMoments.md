# Living Guild Moments

Living Guild moments are environmental storytelling, not notifications.

## Rules

1. Only one notable moment should happen at a time.
2. The room must be enjoyable when absolutely nothing moves.
3. Movement should be slow enough to discover rather than demand attention.
4. Auby's humour should emerge from objects and consequences.
5. Interactive curiosities must never silently change character state.
6. Reduced-motion mode must preserve the complete usable interface.

Future candidates include:

- a biscuit disappearing;
- lantern flame flutter;
- kettle whistle;
- visitor silhouettes;
- seasonal decorations;
- a framed portrait appearing after a campaign;
- the Mini Cheese Dragon stealing something it absolutely should not.
