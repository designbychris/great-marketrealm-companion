# The Guild Illustration Kit

## Purpose

The Guild Illustration Kit is the editable source library behind Generation 2
portraits. The first benchmark is an Apple Fructan Grocer constructed from
fourteen independent SVG assets.

## Quality benchmark

The prototype aims to reproduce the characteristics that make Auby feel alive:

- large glossy eyes;
- rounded silhouettes;
- warm local-colour outlines;
- layered gradients;
- separate painted shadow and highlight passes;
- rosy cheeks;
- tactile cloth and leather;
- small story details;
- warm market-garden lighting.

## Reusability

Race assets own the body and heritage. Class assets own clothing and equipment.
Shared assets own expressions, environments, effects and framing.

The Grocer outfit must therefore remain usable over future compatible Rootkin,
Fungifolk and other race bodies.
