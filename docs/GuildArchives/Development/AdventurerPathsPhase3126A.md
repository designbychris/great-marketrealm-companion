# Phase III.12.6A — The Paladin's Sacred Register

III.12.6A adds the Paladin's read-only Sacred Register and corrects the Grand
Catalogue to the eight Sacred Oaths in the source roadmap.

## Eight Sacred Oaths

The Paladin subclass catalogue now contains:

1. Oath of Inventory
2. Oath of the Colonel
3. Oath of the Creamfather
4. Oath of Aroma
5. Oath of Clearance
6. Oath of Seasoning
7. Oath of Carbonation
8. Oath of the Cleaver Saint

The catalogue snapshot version advances to `3.7.3`, forcing WordPress to
replace the older stored three-Oath snapshot.

All eight Oaths receive creation-time identity, playstyle and best-for
guidance immediately.

Mechanical Oath Gifts remain deferred to III.12.6B.

## Sacred Register

The Paladin Ledger now shows:

- Lay on Hands maximum pool (`5 × Paladin level`);
- Divine Sense maximum uses;
- Charisma-based Sacred Save DC;
- Sacred Aura range;
- Cleansing Touch availability;
- Spellcasting unlock;
- Divine Smite unlock;
- Divine Health;
- Extra Attack;
- Aura of Protection;
- Aura of Courage;
- Improved Divine Smite;
- Sacred Oath status;
- next major sacred milestone.

## Read-only boundary

III.12.6A does not spend or persist:

- Lay on Hands points;
- Divine Sense uses;
- Cleansing Touch uses;
- spell slots;
- Divine Smite.

Those active resources and actions remain assigned to later Paladin phases.
