# Phase III.12.5B — The Monk's Ways

III.12.5B promotes all six repository-defined Monastic Ways into full
specialist progression paths.

## Choice guidance

Every Way now presents four pieces of information before commitment:

- its existing catalogue description;
- specialist identity;
- expected playstyle;
- who that playstyle is best suited to.

The choice card also previews all four future Way Gifts so a player can see
what the character grows toward before choosing.

## Certified Way cadence

Every Monastic Way now grants one automatic gift at:

- Level 3
- Level 6
- Level 11
- Level 17

This follows the cadence reserved by III.12.5.

## Mechanical boundary

These gifts establish identity and certified progression. III.12.5B does not
add new Discipline spend mutations. Any active mechanical techniques powered
by Discipline remain the responsibility of III.12.5C and III.12.5D.
