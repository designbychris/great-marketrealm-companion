# Phase III.12.12A — The Bard's College Register

III.12.12A adds a Bard-specific, read-only College Register to the Arcane
Pantry Ledger.

## Early-level usefulness

Bard College selection occurs at Level 3, but the Register is useful from
Level 1.

A Level 1 Bard sees:

- Bardic Inspiration die and available uses;
- Charisma spell save DC;
- Charisma spell attack bonus;
- spells known;
- cantrips known;
- shared spell slots;
- College selection shown as opening at Level 3;
- the next Calling milestone.

## Bardic Inspiration

The Register displays the Calling progression:

- d6 initially;
- d8 from Level 5;
- d10 from Level 10;
- d12 from Level 15.

Uses are based on Charisma modifier with a minimum usable display pool of one.

Before Level 5, Inspiration refreshes on a long rest.

From Level 5, Font of Inspiration changes that display to short or long rest.

III.12.12A is read-only. Persistent Inspiration expenditure belongs to a later
Bard resource phase.

## Song of Rest

The corrected progression remains:

- Level 2 — d6;
- Level 9 — d8;
- Level 13 — d10;
- Level 17 — d12.

There is no Level 6 increase.

## Bard College

The Register exposes all seven repository-backed Bard College identities.

Before Level 3, the Register explains that College selection is upcoming.

At Level 3 and above, a persisted College is displayed by its certified
catalogue label.

College Gifts remain explicitly pending III.12.12B.

## Spellcasting

The Register reuses shared spell-slot state and displays:

- known-spell model;
- spells known;
- cantrips known;
- maximum spell level;
- Charisma spell save DC;
- Charisma spell attack bonus.

No Bard-specific duplicate slot ledger is introduced.

## Magical Secrets

The Register records the Calling milestones as:

- one pair from Level 10;
- two pairs from Level 14;
- three pairs from Level 18.

Selection UI remains outside this phase.

## Presentation

The College Register uses a subtle performance-parchment treatment while
remaining consistent with the wider Companion Ledger.

It is:

- responsive;
- long-text safe;
- reduced-motion safe;
- forced-colours safe;
- read-only in III.12.12A.
