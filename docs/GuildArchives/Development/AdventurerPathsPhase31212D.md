# Phase III.12.12D — The Bard's Final Certification

III.12.12D certifies the complete Bard Calling built across III.12.12 through
III.12.12C before the project moves to the Artificer's Calling.

## Certified Bard stack

The Final Certification protects:

- specialist Bard advancement;
- Charisma-based known-spell full casting;
- Bardic Inspiration progression;
- Font of Inspiration short-rest recovery threshold;
- Jack of All Trades;
- Song of Rest progression;
- Expertise milestones;
- Countercharm;
- Magical Secrets progression;
- Superior Inspiration;
- Level 3 Bard College selection;
- the seven canon Great Marketrealm Bard Colleges;
- the 26 supplied College Gifts;
- the Bard College Register;
- Living Ledger College Gift presentation;
- source-faithful future College milestone guidance;
- responsive, reduced-motion and forced-colours-safe Ledger presentation.

## Seven sealed Colleges

- College of the Seasoned Song
- College of Nostalgia
- College of Preservation
- Charcutaire
- College of Culinary Crescendo
- College of Confection
- College of Churned Verse

## Source-faithful College cadence

The Final Certification deliberately protects the supplied uneven College
progressions rather than normalizing them into a fabricated shared shape.

- Seasoned Song — Levels 3 / 3 / 6 / 14
- Nostalgia — Levels 3 / 3 / 6
- Preservation — Levels 3 / 3 / 6 / 14
- Charcutaire — Levels 3 / 3 / 6 / 14
- Culinary Crescendo — Levels 3 / 3 / 6 / 14
- Confection — Levels 3 / 6 / 14
- Churned Verse — Levels 3 / 3 / 6 / 14

The shared catalogue therefore contains 26 uniquely keyed Bard College Gifts.

## Nostalgia Level 14 boundary

The supplied College of Nostalgia material ends with Forgotten Favorite at
Level 6.

III.12.12D corrects the generic Bard performance milestone so a Level 13
Nostalgia Bard is told that Level 14 brings Magical Secrets, but is not told
that an unsupplied Final College Gift exists.

The Living College Gifts surface likewise reports the Nostalgia progression as
complete once its supplied gifts are available.

No Level 14 Nostalgia capstone is invented for symmetry.

## Bardic performance thresholds

Bardic Inspiration remains:

- d6 before Level 5;
- d8 from Level 5;
- d10 from Level 10;
- d12 from Level 15.

Font of Inspiration changes its refresh cadence from long rest to short or
long rest at Level 5.

Song of Rest remains:

- unavailable before Level 2;
- d6 from Level 2;
- d8 from Level 9;
- d10 from Level 13;
- d12 from Level 17.

## Spellcasting

Bard remains a Charisma-based known-spell full caster and reaches ninth-circle
magic at Level 17.

The current spell catalogue names remain part of the wider Companion spell
system. The separately planned post-Calling handbook pass will reconcile the
renamed Great Marketrealm spells after all Calling work is complete.

## Resource boundary

This Final Certification does not invent persistent Bardic Inspiration
expenditure or a separate Bard resource ledger.

The certified Bard Register remains read-only for Inspiration capacity and
refresh cadence. Finite active-play tracking can be introduced later if a
separate resource slice is desired, without changing the certified progression
or College mechanics.

## Living Ledger

The Bard College Register and Living College Gifts remain two projections of
the same certified Calling data:

- the Register summarizes current Bard performance and the next Calling
  milestone;
- the Living College Gifts surface shows only College mechanics supplied and
  unlocked at the current Bard level;
- future College Gifts are derived from the shared Path Gift catalogue rather
  than duplicated in the view.

## Browser hardening

The Final Certification protects the Bard Ledger's existing:

- long-label wrapping;
- narrow-screen stacking;
- responsive College Gift grids;
- reduced-motion compatibility;
- forced-colours visibility.

The Bard is therefore certified as a complete specialist Calling at the end of
III.12.12D, ready for browser verification before III.12.13 — The Artificer's
Calling.
