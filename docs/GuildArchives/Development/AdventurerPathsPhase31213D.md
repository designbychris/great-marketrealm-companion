# Phase III.12.13D — The Artificer’s Final Certification

## Guild Seal

**Status:** Implemented — awaiting server PHPUnit and browser certification.

Phase III.12.13D is the final Artificer seal and the closing certification
slice for the thirteen specialist Callings covered by Phase III.12.

## Artificer Final Seal

The certification suite protects the complete Artificer stack:

- the specialist Artificer advancement spine;
- Magical Tinkering and Intelligence-based prepared spellcasting foundations;
- Infuse Item, The Right Tool for the Job, Tool Expertise, Flash of Genius,
  Magic Item Adept, Spell-Storing Item, Magic Item Savant, Magic Item Master
  and Soul of Artifice;
- prepared half-caster spell-circle progression through 5th-level spells;
- the Level 3 Artificer Specialisation boundary;
- The Spice Engineer, The Cheesemonger, The Sous-Sorcerer and The Culinary
  Engineer;
- all 17 supplied Specialisation Gifts;
- source-faithful Specialisation Gift cadences;
- Living Ledger Specialisation and Gift projections;
- responsive, reduced-motion and forced-colour browser hardening.

## Source-Fidelity Exception

The Sous-Sorcerer remains intentionally shorter than the other supplied
Specialisations. The Player’s Handbook supplies its Level 3 core package and
Flavour Surge but no Level 5, 9 or 15 Specialisation Gifts.

The final certification therefore verifies that neither the Register nor the
Living Ledger invents those missing features.
