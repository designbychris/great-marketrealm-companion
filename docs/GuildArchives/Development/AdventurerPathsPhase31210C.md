# Phase III.12.10C — The Druid's Wild Shape & Primal Reserves

III.12.10C adds persistent active-play expenditure for core Wild Shape and
the finite Circle abilities explicitly defined in the supplied Druid canon.

## Wild Shape

Wild Shape becomes available at Level 2 with two uses per short or long rest.
At Level 20, Archdruid makes Wild Shape unlimited.

The active resource ledger stores expenditure only; progression owns the
maximum.

## Circle reserves

Only abilities with explicit finite-use language receive counters.

### Circle of Eating Fresh

- Crisp Aura Expansion — once per long rest.
- Preservative Purge — once per long rest from Level 14.

### Circle of the Groveflame

- Spice Basilisk Breath — once per short rest from Level 10.
- Scorching Bloom — once per long rest from Level 14.
- Pungent Flame — once per short or long rest from Level 14.

### Circle of the Deep Soil

- Living Earthquake — once per long rest from Level 14.

### Circle of the Compost

- Compost Surge — proficiency bonus uses per long rest.
- Mulchborn — once per short rest.
- Bloom of Decay — once per long rest.
- Blight Circle Grant — once per long rest without a spell slot.
- Insect Plague Circle Grant — once per long rest without a spell slot.

Avatar of the Rotten Grove does not get a second counter because the supplied
feature explicitly spends Wild Shape.

### Circle of Curdle

- Animate Spoil — once per long rest.

Bacteria Bloom receives no invented use limit because none was supplied.

### Circle of the Churn

- Frozen Curd free use — once per long rest, kept separate from the option to
  expend Wild Shape.
- Glacial Growth — proficiency bonus uses per long rest.
- True Churnform — once per long rest.

## Rest ownership

A Primal Short Rest restores only Wild Shape and abilities explicitly marked
for short-rest recovery.

A Primal Long Rest restores all Primal Reserves. The controller then restores
shared Druid spell slots separately, preserving clean resource ownership.

## Active-play boundary

III.12.10C tracks expenditure. It does not yet perform transformations, roll
Circle damage/healing, summon creatures, apply terrain, or execute other
Circle actions. Those belong to III.12.10D — The Druid's Grove Arts.
