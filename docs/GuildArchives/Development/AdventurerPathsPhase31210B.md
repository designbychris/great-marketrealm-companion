# Phase III.12.10B — The Druid's Circles

This slice certifies the six supplied Marketrealm Druid Circles.

## Certified Circles

- Circle of Eating Fresh
- Circle of the Groveflame
- Circle of the Deep Soil
- Circle of the Compost
- Circle of Curdle
- Circle of the Churn

Each Circle now has its authoritative Level 2 / 6 / 10 / 14 gifts in the
shared Path Gift catalogue and choice-guide metadata for the Circle Grove
Register.

Where the supplied source defines multiple features at one milestone, they
remain together at that milestone.

## Source fidelity

Mechanics supplied for the setting are preserved rather than normalised.
Examples include Crisp Aura's 1 HP per round, Living Earthquake's fixed DC 16,
Rot Within's -1 AC, and True Churnform's maximized healing/cold spells.

## Circle of the Churn spells

The supplied 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 Druid-level Circle Spell table is represented in a
dedicated Druid Circle spell catalogue. No spell grants are invented for the
other five Circles because none were supplied.

## Active-play boundary

This phase certifies progression data only. PB/long-rest counters, short-rest
recharges, Wild Shape expenditure, transformation actions, and other active
Circle controls remain for later Druid reserve/arts phases.
