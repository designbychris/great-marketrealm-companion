# Phase III.12.6 — The Paladin's Calling

Phase III.12.6 promotes Paladin from the generic registered-Calling fallback
to its own specialist 1–20 advancement definition.

## Level-one foundations

Paladin creation retains two Calling foundations:

- Divine Sense
- Lay on Hands

The persistent Lay on Hands reserve is deliberately deferred to the Sacred
Register / Sacred Reserves work rather than being invented in this foundation
slice.

## Advancement spine

The Calling now records the Paladin-owned milestones:

- Level 2 — Fighting Style, Spellcasting, Divine Smite
- Level 3 — Divine Health
- Level 5 — Extra Attack
- Level 6 — Aura of Protection (10 ft)
- Level 10 — Aura of Courage (10 ft)
- Level 11 — Improved Divine Smite
- Level 14 — Cleansing Touch
- Level 18 — aura range improves to 30 ft

The shared Measure of Growth remains delegated at Levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 19.

## Sacred Oath boundary

Sacred Oath selection is reserved at Level 3.

Oath-owned gifts are delegated through the existing Path Gifts boundary at
Levels 3, 7, 15 and 20.

III.12.6 does not invent the oath catalogue. That belongs to III.12.6B, where
the Great Marketrealm Oaths can receive the same identity, playstyle,
best-for and gift-preview guidance already established for other Callings.

## Spellcasting boundary

Level 2 correctly records Spellcasting as a Paladin milestone, but this phase
does not pretend that the existing Wizard-only spellcasting progression
catalogue already supports Paladin.

The Paladin half-caster spell-slot progression will be certified in the
appropriate Paladin register/spellcasting slice.

## Active-play boundary

This phase records Divine Smite, Lay on Hands, auras and Cleansing Touch as
Calling progression only.

It does not yet:

- spend Lay on Hands points;
- consume spell slots for Divine Smite;
- infer a qualifying melee hit;
- automate aura target eligibility;
- spend Cleansing Touch uses.

Those belong to the later Paladin active-play phases.
