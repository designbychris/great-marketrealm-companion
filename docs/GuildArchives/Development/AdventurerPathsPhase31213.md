# Phase III.12.13 — The Artificer’s Calling

## Final Calling foundation

Phase III.12.13 promotes the Artificer from the generic registered Calling
fallback into a specialist progression definition.

The class keeps its existing certified identity — d8 Hit Die, Constitution and
Intelligence saving throws, Intelligence-driven Arcane Pantry support and the
existing Artificer spell hooks — while gaining its permanent advancement spine.

### Certified foundations

- Magical Tinkering
- prepared Intelligence-based spellcasting
- Infuse Item
- The Right Tool for the Job
- Tool Expertise
- Flash of Genius
- Magic Item Adept
- Spell-Storing Item
- Magic Item Savant
- Magic Item Master
- Soul of Artifice

Artificer spellcasting is represented as prepared half-casting, reaching fifth-
level spells at Artificer level 17. Cantrips progress from two to three at level
10 and four at level 14.

### Specialisation boundary

Specialisation Gifts are deliberately delegated at levels 3, 5, 9 and 15.
Their identities and mechanics are not duplicated in the base Calling. They
belong to the dedicated III.12.13A/III.12.13B slices and the Great Marketrealm
Players Handbook remains the canonical mechanics source.

### Growth boundary

Levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 19 continue to delegate ability improvement/talent work
to The Measure of Growth (III.8.10).

## Regression seal

The Artificer Calling regression suite protects the specialist catalogue
registration, foundations, core inventor milestones, growth and specialisation
boundaries, prepared half-caster model, cantrip thresholds, fifth-circle ceiling,
capability audit, existing class identity and foreign-Calling guards.
