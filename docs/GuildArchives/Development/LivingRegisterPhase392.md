# Character Lifecycle Initiative — Phase III.9.2
## Fresh Ink in the Living Register

III.9.2 makes the Living Register visibly respond to completed Guild Certification.

The current-state summary from III.9.1 remains authoritative. A new **Fresh Ink** projection reads the latest immutable advancement-history entry and explains the most recently sealed change.

For a Wizard certification this can include:

- the newly certified level;
- maximum HP gained and the before/after vitality total;
- spells entered into the permanent Spellbook;
- cantrips entered into the permanent Spellbook;
- Gifts of the Path granted by the newly reached level.

Fresh Ink is presentation only. It does not persist a second copy of progression state, mutate the Character, inspect pending advancement paperwork, or alter certification rules.

This creates the first temporal layer of the Living Register: III.9.1 answers **what is true now**, while III.9.2 also answers **what just became true**.

That distinction is intended to support later Register history and Guild Diceworks surfaces without coupling live-play systems to the advancement workflow.
