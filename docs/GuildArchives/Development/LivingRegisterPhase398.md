# Phase III.9.8 — Living Register Presentation & Accessibility Refinement

This phase closes the Living Register presentation pass without adding new progression state.

## Refinements

- The Living Register heading now explicitly describes the section introduction for assistive technology.
- Long certified histories receive a compact Register index linking directly to Fresh Ink, the Journey Measure, the Record of Change and the Sealed Chronicle.
- Linked sections are programmatically focusable and receive visible focus treatment.
- Then-to-now comparisons expose a spoken **to** rather than relying on a decorative arrow.
- The latest Chronicle entry is identified in text as **Latest certification**, not by styling alone.
- Level 20 no longer renders a contradictory **Next Guild Certification** preview.
- Chronicle headers and dates reflow more cleanly on narrow screens.
- Focus treatment remains usable with reduced-motion preferences and does not require animation.

## Boundary

III.9.8 changes presentation only. The Living Register remains a read-only projection of certified progression state.
