# Character Lifecycle Initiative — Phase III.4
## The Clash of the Ledger

Equipped weapons now become attack entries in the Open Ledger. Attack bonuses use the relevant ability modifier plus proficiency; finesse weapons choose Dexterity when it is better. Damage uses the weapon die plus the same ability modifier.

The Guild Dice now supports contextual attack rolls and arbitrary `NdN` weapon damage formulae. Natural 20 attack rolls announce a critical hit and remind the player to double the weapon dice. Natural 1 retains Auby’s unfortunate-attempt treatment.

The combat presenter is deliberately separate from inventory persistence so future unarmed strikes, spells, class features and magical weapons can join the attack list without rebuilding the Adventurer’s Pack.
