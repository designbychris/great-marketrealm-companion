# Phase III.17 — The Keeper's Workshop

## III.17.1 — The Dungeon Master's Desk Grows

Phase III.17 begins by extending the certified Dungeon Master's Desk rather than creating a second DM workspace.

### Foundation contract

- Existing Campaign, Session, Encounter, Roster, Monster and Journal ledgers remain unchanged and open.
- `KeeperWorkspace` is the presentation/catalogue seam for forthcoming preparation instruments.
- `PlanningRecordType` establishes stable vocabulary for `location`, `person`, `faction`, `evidence`, and `thread` records.
- No new persistence or database schema is introduced in III.17.1.
- No dead routes are advertised. Planned instruments are visibly marked as planned rather than linked to unfinished screens.
- Canonical campaign records will remain the source of truth. Visual boards and future Tabletop scenes reference those records instead of duplicating them.

### Planned continuation

1. **III.17.2 — The Keeper's Gazetteer** — campaign locations.
2. **III.17.3 — Dramatis Personae** — campaign people and factions.
3. **III.17.4 — The Evidence Register** — clues, secrets and plot threads.
4. **III.17.5 — The Conspiracy Board** — visual arrangement and relationships across those records.
5. **IV.36 — The Cartographer's Bench** — a later Companion ↔ Tabletop location/scene bridge and diorama map authoring system.

The Cartographer remains deliberately behind the GMRT boundary: GMRC owns what a campaign location *means*; GMRT will own how its playable scene *works*.
