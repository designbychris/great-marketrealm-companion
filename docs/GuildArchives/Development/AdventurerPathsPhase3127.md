# Phase III.12.7 — The Warlock's Calling

III.12.7 promotes Warlock from the generic registered-Calling fallback to its
own specialist advancement definition.

## Source boundary

The fresh repository already contained:

- Warlock as a Charisma Calling;
- Warlock-specific Pact Magic slot handling in the Arcane Pantry;
- the Marketrealm cantrip `Bureaucratic Hex`;
- four registered Warlock Patron paths:
  - Pact of the Mascot
  - The Forgotten Freezer
  - The Spoilfather
  - The Sugar Fiend

The detailed Calling spine added by III.12.7 follows the same 2014-style class
progression family already represented by the project's Fighter, Rogue, Monk
and Paladin implementations.

Patron-specific gift mechanics are not invented in this phase.

## Level-one foundations

Warlock creation now has two explicit Calling foundations:

- Pact Magic
- Otherworldly Patron

The Patron path is a Level 1 choice and is registered with the shared
`PathProgressionCatalogue` as the `Patron Contract Folio`.

## Calling-owned progression

III.12.7 records:

- Level 2 — Eldritch Invocations (2)
- Level 3 — Pact Boon; 2nd-level Pact slots
- Level 5 — 3 Invocations; 3rd-level Pact slots
- Level 7 — 4 Invocations; 4th-level Pact slots
- Level 9 — 5 Invocations; 5th-level Pact slots
- Level 11 — 6th-level Mystic Arcanum; 3 Pact slots
- Level 12 — 6 Invocations
- Level 13 — 7th-level Mystic Arcanum
- Level 15 — 7 Invocations; 8th-level Mystic Arcanum
- Level 17 — 9th-level Mystic Arcanum; 4 Pact slots
- Level 18 — 8 Invocations
- Level 20 — Eldritch Master

The shared Measure of Growth remains delegated at Levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 19.

## Patron boundary

The four Patron identities are visible now, but their specialist gifts remain
reserved for III.12.7B.

Later Patron Gifts are delegated at Levels 6, 10 and 14. The initial Patron
identity begins at Level 1.

## Pact Magic boundary

Pact Magic remains deliberately distinct from the Wizard-style
`SpellcastingProgressionCatalogue`.

III.12.7 records its advancement spine but does not yet:

- persist Pact slot expenditure;
- restore Pact slots on a short rest;
- implement spell-known choices;
- implement Invocation choices;
- implement Mystic Arcanum selection/use;
- cast Warlock spells from the Ledger.

Those belong to the later Warlock slices.
