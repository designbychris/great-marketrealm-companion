# Phase III.9.7 — Living Register Hardening & Polish

This phase strengthens the Living Register against sparse, legacy and edge-case character records without adding new mutable progression state.

## Hardening

- Certification history is normalised before chronological logic, so sparse array keys and malformed legacy debris cannot corrupt sequence numbers, latest-entry detection, certification totals or milestone chronology.
- Spell and cantrip choices are recognised by semantic `-spells` and `-cantrips` suffixes rather than Wizard-only keys, keeping Fresh Ink compatible with future caster Callings.
- Empty certification history receives an explicit Register standing and a friendly first-seal empty state.
- Level 20 characters receive a final-seal standing rather than language implying another advancement awaits.
- Characters without Paths, Path Gifts or spellcasting continue to render valid zero/empty certified state.

## Boundaries

The Living Register remains read-only. Current HP and temporary HP remain mutable Adventuring Measures; only certified maximum-HP growth belongs to progression history.
