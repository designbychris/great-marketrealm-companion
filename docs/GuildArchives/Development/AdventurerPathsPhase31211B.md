# Phase III.12.11B — The Cleric's Sacred Domains

III.12.11B certifies the six Great Marketrealm Cleric Divine Domains and
normalizes the older, uneven Domain drafts onto the Cleric's established
1 / 2 / 6 / 8 / 17 specialist cadence.

## Design rule

Supplied mechanics remain authoritative where they are complete.

Where an older Domain was missing one or more normal Cleric milestones, this
phase adds a restrained theme-matching feature rather than leaving that Domain
materially weaker than newer options.

Editorial additions are marked with `editorial => true` in the gift catalogue
so later balancing work can distinguish original source features from the
normalization pass.

## Domain of Sweetness

The supplied progression is already complete and remains source-faithful:

- Level 1 — Bonus Cantrips & Sweet Sanctuary
- Level 2 — Channel Divinity: Sugarburst
- Level 6 — Sticky Ward
- Level 8 — Divine Strike: Sticky Smite
- Level 17 — Ascension of the Sugarcloud

Its complete supplied Domain Spell table is preserved.

## Domain of the Golden Arches

The older source had Level 1, Channel Divinity and Level 17 concepts but no
Level 6 or Level 8 feature.

Normalized progression:

- Level 1 — Divine Combo Meal & Sacred Sauce
- Level 2 — Channel Divinity: Order Up
- Level 6 — Express Blessing (editorial)
- Level 8 — Divine Strike: Golden Fry (editorial)
- Level 17 — Happy Heal Hour

Happy Heal Hour is clarified into a once-per-long-rest, one-minute window in
which healing spells restore maximum possible hit points.

Only the supplied signature spells are registered:

- Level 1 — Grease
- Level 5 — Create Food and Water

A full invented spell table is deliberately not fabricated.

## Domain of Dairy

The source called Grease a “Bonus Cantrip”. Grease is a 1st-level spell, so
the terminology is corrected without removing it from the supplied Dairy
Domain Spell table.

Curdled Blessing explicitly spends Channel Divinity, so it is normalized from
the source's Level 1 placement to Level 2, when Clerics actually gain Channel
Divinity.

Normalized progression:

- Level 1 — Dairy Domain Magic
- Level 2 — Channel Divinity: Curdled Blessing
- Level 6 — Stinky Salvation
- Level 8 — Divine Strike: Cultured Smite (editorial)
- Level 17 — Holy Butterstorm

## Domain of Seasoning

The supplied Level 1 / 2 / 6 / 8 progression is preserved.

The missing capstone is completed with:

- Level 17 — Perfect Balance (editorial)

Perfect Balance allows Cleric spell fire, poison and acid damage to ignore
resistance for one minute once per long rest. Immunity is explicitly not
bypassed.

## Domain of Cultivation

The original draft had a strong theme and Domain Spell table but only
proficiencies and Blessed Brine as class features.

The completed progression is:

- Level 1 — Cultivator Proficiencies
- Level 2 — Channel Divinity: Blessed Brine
- Level 6 — Patient Culture (editorial)
- Level 8 — Potent Spellcasting: Cultivated Faith (editorial)
- Level 17 — Sacred Vintage (editorial)

The additions intentionally emphasize patience, restoration, poison/disease
protection and mature spellcraft so Cultivation remains distinct from the much
more aggressive Domain of Fermentation.

## Domain of Fermentation

The supplied progression is already rich and complete:

- Level 1 — Ferment Touch & Bonus Proficiencies
- Level 2 — Channel Divinity: Funk of the Divine
- Level 6 — Spiritual Brine
- Level 8 — Pickled Spirits
- Level 17 — Mother Culture

Its scaling Ferment Touch, 2d10 + Cleric-level Funk damage, poison-to-healing
reaction, temporary-HP support and Mother Culture effects are preserved.

## Domain spell tables

Complete supplied 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 tables are registered for:

- Sweetness
- Dairy
- Seasoning
- Cultivation
- Fermentation

Golden Arches remains intentionally partial because the source supplied only
Grease and Create Food and Water as signature spells.

## Sacred Domain Register

The existing III.12.11A presenter now reports:

- certified Domain Gift count;
- supplied Domain Spell table;
- currently unlocked Domain Spells.

The stale III.12.11 and III.12.11A tests that protected the old “Domain Gifts
do not exist yet” boundary are updated to the new certified state.

## Active-play boundary

This phase certifies Domain progression and spell data.

It does not yet spend Channel Divinity, Ferment Touch uses, Stinky Salvation,
weapon Zest, Happy Heal Hour, Ascension, Mother Culture or other finite
resources.

Those belong to the Cleric Sacred Reserves and Divine Arts slices.

## Broader class consistency

This pass used the current repository as the authority and deliberately
avoided unrelated cross-Calling balance rewrites.

Where later class reviews reveal an obvious terminology or progression error,
the same policy applies:

1. preserve established Marketrealm identity;
2. correct objective rules/terminology mistakes;
3. fill genuine missing framework milestones conservatively;
4. mark editorial additions transparently;
5. avoid silently replacing homebrew mechanics with generic D&D rules.
