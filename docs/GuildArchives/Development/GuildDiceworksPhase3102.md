# Phase III.10.2 — The Guild Dice Set

The Guild Diceworks now presents the standard tabletop die families as distinct visual dice and supports pools of more than one die.

## Standard Guild set

- d4
- d6
- d8
- d10
- d12
- d20
- d100

Each family has its own silhouette in the Diceworks tray. Formula-driven rolls render every die individually, so `2d6` produces two d6s and `4d8` produces four d8s. Pools larger than four dice switch to a compact grid; very large pools receive a denser layout.

## Free Roll

The tray now includes a keyboard-accessible Guild Free Roll disclosure with:

- quantity from 1 to 20;
- standard die selection;
- modifier from -99 to +99;
- a Roll Dice action.

The free roller uses the same secure random source, result presentation, recent-roll history and live announcements as character-driven rolls.

## D20 reactions

Natural reactions remain a d20 concern. A free `1d20` can receive the Natural 20 celebration or Natural 1 lonely-confetti response. Multi-d20 pools do not infer a critical outcome from one member of the pool.

## Advantage and disadvantage

Advantage and disadvantage render both d20s and visually mark the kept die. The arithmetic and accessible text continue to state the selected natural result.

## Boundaries

The Guild Dice Set remains a presentation/play utility. It does not mutate Character state, HP, progression, certification history, or the Living Register.
