# IV.34.2 — The Table Remembers Tonight

The Companion now owns the stable Campaign ↔ Tabletop relationship while the Tabletop owns the factual play-session lifecycle.

A linked Tabletop Session is upserted into the existing Dungeon Master's Session Ledger. The bridge records immutable Tabletop IDs, actual start/end timestamps and calculated duration without replacing private preparation notes, recap text or attendance already held by the Companion record. A live Tabletop Session is presented as **In Progress** and becomes **Played** when the Keeper ends it.

The existing Campaign ↔ Fellowship relationship is projected to the Tabletop so Pippin's Table Atlas can identify the company attached to the Campaign. Publishing the Session's safe public/shared history into the Company Chronicle remains a separate IV.34.3 boundary, preventing DM-private Ledger fields from being copied into Fellowship history.
