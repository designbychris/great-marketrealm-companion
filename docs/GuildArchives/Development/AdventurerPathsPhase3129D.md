# Phase III.12.9D — The Ranger's Field Arts

III.12.9D turns the eight certified Ranger Paths into player-facing active
technique surfaces.

## Source-faithful rule

The Field Arts layer uses only numerical formulas and options explicitly
supplied in the Ranger canon.

It does not invent:

- Forager remedy preparation counts;
- Nettle Oil damage dice;
- Seedshot ammunition limits;
- Burstmelon damage dice;
- Bloom Seed healing amounts;
- Whiteout Hunter damage dice beyond the supplied proficiency-bonus wording.

Those mechanics remain descriptive until the source material defines them.

## Aislewarden Conclave

Mark the Spoor exposes its supplied quarry damage roll:

- 1d6 from Level 3;
- 1d8 from Level 11.

Mobility and pursuit features remain clearly described.

## Deep-Root Warden

Field Arts expose:

- Grasping Roots with its III.12.9C proficiency-bonus reserve;
- Rooted Defence;
- Thornroad with its supplied 1d6 piercing roll;
- Heart of the Rootlands with its once-per-long-rest reserve and supplied
  2d8 healing roll, with Wisdom modifier explicitly remaining additional.

## Cold Vault Stalker

The surface exposes:

- Frost-Tipped Hunter — 1d6 cold;
- Flash Freeze;
- Shattering Shot — 2d6 cold;
- Whiteout Hunter.

## Conclave of the Forager

The five supplied remedies are presented separately:

- Mintleaf Draught;
- Basil Balm;
- Rosemary Tonic;
- Nettle Oil;
- Sagebrew.

Sagebrew can roll its supplied 1d4 check bonus. Nettle Oil remains descriptive
because the supplied source does not define its poison damage die.

Miracle Harvest reuses the III.12.9C once-per-long-rest reserve.

## Spice Trail Hunter

Chilli, Ginger, Garlic and Pepperleaf appear as separate elemental choices.

Their damage progresses from 1d6 to 2d6 at Level 11.

The Final Seasoning keeps fire, thunder, radiant and poison as four separate
2d6 rolls so the damage types remain visible instead of being collapsed into
one anonymous 8d6 result.

Its once-per-long-rest use reuses the existing Field Reserve.

## Rindrunner

Piercing Wedge receives its supplied 1d8 roll.

Ancient Rind reuses its Wisdom-modifier Field Reserve.

## Seedshot Conclave

The five supplied Seedshots appear as separate choices:

- Vine Seed;
- Burstmelon Seed;
- Sunseed;
- Heavyseed;
- Bloom Seed.

No missing damage or healing formulas are fabricated.

Ancient Seed reuses its once-per-long-rest reserve.

## Expiry Hunter

Expiry Mark exposes separate 1d8 radiant and 1d8 necrotic choices.

Put It Back reuses its proficiency-bonus Field Reserve.

## Diceworks

Field Art rolls use the shared Guild Diceworks trigger rather than adding a
Ranger-specific roller.

## Accessibility

The Field Arts surface uses native buttons and server-backed forms, stacks on
small screens, permits long labels to wrap and retains forced-colours support.
