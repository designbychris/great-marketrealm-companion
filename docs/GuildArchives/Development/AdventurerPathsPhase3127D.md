# Phase III.12.7D — The Warlock's Eldritch Arts

III.12.7D turns the Warlock's signature at-will attack into a correct
active-play action surface.

## Repository terminology

The current Great Marketrealm Arcane Ability Catalogue names the Warlock's
signature force cantrip `Bureaucratic Hex`.

III.12.7D preserves that name rather than adding a duplicate Eldritch Blast
entry.

## Independent beam progression

Bureaucratic Hex now resolves as independent beams:

- Levels 1–4 — 1 beam
- Levels 5–10 — 2 beams
- Levels 11–16 — 3 beams
- Levels 17–20 — 4 beams

Every beam:

- makes its own Charisma + proficiency spell attack;
- deals `1d10` force damage on a hit;
- may choose its own creature target;
- does not consume a Pact Magic slot.

The Ledger deliberately does not combine multiple beams into one `2d10`,
`3d10` or `4d10` damage roll.

## Guild Diceworks

Each beam has separate:

- Roll Beam Attack
- Roll Beam Damage

controls using the existing Guild Diceworks contracts.

This preserves independent targeting, attack rolls, critical handling and
damage application.

## Arcane Pantry compatibility

The generic Arcane Ability Catalogue still contains its compact
character-level scaling description.

When the Character is a Warlock and the ability is `bureaucratic-hex`, the
generic Arcane card suppresses its combined-dice roll controls and directs the
player to the dedicated Eldritch Arts surface.

This avoids presenting two contradictory active-play methods.

## Invocation boundary

III.12.7D does not assume any Eldritch Invocation has been selected.

No Agonizing Blast-style damage modifier, forced movement or range enhancement
is invented here.

Those require a real Invocation choice system in a later Warlock slice.

## Pact Magic boundary

The repository currently contains no numbered Warlock spell entries beyond
Bureaucratic Hex.

III.12.7D therefore does not fabricate a Warlock spell list.

The III.12.7C Pact Reserve controls remain available for recording Pact Magic
slot expenditure until named Warlock spells are added to the Arcane Catalogue.
