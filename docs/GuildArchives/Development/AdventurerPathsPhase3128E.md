# Phase III.12.8E — The Sorcerer's Final Seal

III.12.8E certifies the Sorcerer Calling as currently implemented across
III.12.8, III.12.8A, III.12.8C and III.12.8D.

## Certified Sorcerer stack

The Final Seal protects:

- specialist Sorcerer advancement;
- Charisma-based full spellcasting;
- known-spells progression through Level 20;
- Level 1 Sorcerous Origin choice;
- five registered Marketrealm Sorcerous Origins;
- Origin Spark Register;
- Level 2 Font of Magic;
- persistent Sorcery Point expenditure;
- Sorcery Point maximum equal to Sorcerer level;
- Flexible Casting in both directions;
- Level 1–5 spell-slot creation costs of 2 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 7 points;
- long-rest restoration of Sorcery Points and standard spell slots;
- Metamagic allowance of 2 / 3 / 4 options at Levels 3 / 10 / 17;
- eight certified Metamagic options;
- persistent owner-scoped Metamagic selections;
- active Metamagic Sorcery Point expenditure;
- responsive and forced-colours-safe Ledger presentation.

## Explicit Origin Gift boundary

Phase III.12.8B was not built before the Final Seal.

The five Sorcerous Origins therefore remain registered path identities but do
not yet have automatic `PathGiftCatalogue` definitions.

The Final Seal intentionally protects that truth. It does not invent Origin
Gift features or pretend that they are currently certified.

A future Origin Gifts expansion can register those gifts cleanly without
changing the Sorcerer's already-certified Font of Magic or Metamagic systems.

## Final browser hardening

The Final Seal adds defensive layout rules for long Metamagic names, timing
guidance, variable-cost controls and action buttons.

Buttons remain within their cards, labels may wrap naturally, and very narrow
screens stack technique headers instead of forcing overflow.

Native checkboxes, selects and server-backed forms remain keyboard operable
without JavaScript.

## Resource ownership

Permanent progression owns maximums and allowances.

Durable character choice persistence owns selected Metamagic Arts.

The shared active resource ledger stores only expenditure.

This distinction remains the core Sorcerer accounting contract.
