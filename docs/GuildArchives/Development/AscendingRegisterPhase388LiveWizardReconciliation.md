# Phase III.8.8 — Live Wizard Advancement Reconciliation

The live Level 2 Wizard test exposed a spell-catalogue gap rather than a Path
problem.

The Wizard already has **School of Shelfmancy** permanently certified, so no
new Arcane Tradition choice should appear during ordinary advancement.

At Level 2 the Wizard had already learned the only two existing Level-1 Wizard
spells:

- Pantry Ward
- Market Missile

The Arcane Pantry now adds four Level-2 Wizard studies:

- Aisle Step
- Stockroom Veil
- Price Freeze
- Crate Levitation

A Level 2 Wizard advancing to Level 3 therefore receives a real choose-two
Spellbook Folio. Two choices remain available for the Level 4 advancement.

Spell progression also no longer silently reduces a Calling rule merely
because the current catalogue has too few options. If a future level asks for
more spells than the Pantry contains, the Spellbook Folio remains in Attention
and explicitly reports the catalogue shortfall.

Expected Level 2 → 3 behaviour for the existing Shelfmancy Wizard:

1. No new Arcane Tradition Folio.
2. School of Shelfmancy remains certified.
3. Spellbook Folio offers four Level-2 spells.
4. Choose any two spells.
5. Record the Vitality choice.
6. Every current folio becomes Ready.
7. Auby issues the Advancement Seal.
8. Guild Certification advances the Character to Level 3.
9. The chosen spells persist in the permanent Spellbook.
