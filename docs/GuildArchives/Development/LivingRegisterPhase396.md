# Phase III.9.6 — The Living Record of Change

The Living Register now presents a read-only **then → now** account of certified character growth.

## Purpose

The Sealed Chronicle records each certification and the Measure of the Journey totals what it carried. The Living Record of Change answers a different question: **how has this adventurer changed from the earliest retained Guild seal to their current certified state?**

## Derived record

The presenter derives:

- earliest retained certified level → current level;
- starting maximum HP → current maximum HP when the archive contains the original maximum;
- certified maximum-HP growth as a safe fallback for older archive entries;
- the first certification that records a Calling Path;
- the first certification that grants a Path Gift;
- the first certification that expands spells or cantrips.

No new persistence is introduced. Current and temporary HP remain Adventuring Measures and are not historical progression state.

## Boundary

The Rising Register still owns pending advancement. The Living Record reads only completed certification history plus current permanent Character state.
