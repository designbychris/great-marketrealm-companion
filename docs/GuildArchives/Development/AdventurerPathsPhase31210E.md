# Phase III.12.10E — The Druid's Final Seal

III.12.10E certifies the complete Druid Calling built across III.12.10 through
III.12.10D.

## Certified stack

The Final Seal protects:

- specialist Druid advancement;
- Druidic and prepared Wisdom spellcasting;
- full-caster spell-circle progression;
- Level 2 Wild Shape and Circle selection;
- Circle Grove Register;
- six Marketrealm Druid Circles;
- Circle Gifts at Levels 2 / 6 / 10 / 14;
- Wild Shape and Primal Reserves;
- short-rest and long-rest ownership;
- Grove Arts;
- shared Guild Diceworks integration;
- responsive, reduced-motion and forced-colours-safe Ledger presentation.

## Six sealed Circles

- Circle of Eating Fresh
- Circle of the Groveflame
- Circle of the Deep Soil
- Circle of the Compost
- Circle of Curdle
- Circle of the Churn

## Marketrealm source fidelity

The Final Seal protects intentionally unusual mechanics from being silently
normalised by later refactors.

Examples include:

- Crisp Aura restoring exactly 1 HP per round;
- Living Earthquake retaining its supplied fixed DC 16;
- Rot Within imposing -1 AC;
- True Churnform maximizing healing and cold spell results;
- Compost Surge dealing static necrotic damage equal to Druid level;
- Bacteria Bloom receiving no invented resource limit;
- Avatar of the Rotten Grove spending Wild Shape rather than gaining a second
  fabricated counter.

## Wild Shape

Wild Shape remains two uses per short or long rest from Level 2.

At Level 20, Archdruid makes the Wild Shape reserve unlimited.

Circle forms which explicitly spend Wild Shape reuse that same reserve.

## Circle of the Compost

The Final Seal specifically protects the active-play chain used by the current
Compost test character:

- Rotbound Affinity;
- Compost Surge with PB-per-long-rest uses;
- Reclaim Vitality at 1d6 + Wisdom modifier;
- Recycle into Harm as static Druid-level necrotic damage;
- Mulchborn at 2d8 poison with its calculated save DC;
- Bloom of Decay at 4d6 poison and 1d6 ally healing;
- slot-free Blight and Insect Plague resources;
- Avatar of the Rotten Grove using Wild Shape and split Mulch Slam damage.

## Circle spell boundary

Only Circle of the Churn has a supplied Circle Spell table. Its exact
3 / 5 / 7 / 9 progression remains certified.

No equivalent spell tables are invented for the other five Circles.

## Rest ownership

DruidPrimalReserveService owns Druid Primal expenditure and recovery.

Short rests restore Wild Shape and explicitly short-rest-refreshing Circle
features.

Long rests restore all Primal Reserves. Shared spell slots remain owned by
SharedSpellSlotReserveService and are restored separately by the complete
controller long-rest flow.

## Diceworks

Grove Arts reuse the shared Guild Diceworks contract. No Druid-specific dice
engine exists.

Formula, modifier, kind and damage type remain separate pieces of roll data,
while static source outcomes stay static.

## Browser hardening

The Final Seal reinforces:

- long-label wrapping;
- minimum comfortable action heights;
- very narrow-screen stacking;
- reduced-motion compatibility;
- forced-colours button visibility.

The Druid is therefore certified as a complete specialist Calling at the end
of III.12.10E.
