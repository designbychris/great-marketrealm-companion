# The Great Marketrealm Companion Manifesto

*"Every adventurer deserves to be remembered."*

---

# Why We Exist

The Great Marketrealm Companion is not simply a character creator.

It is the Guild Archives.

Every adventurer who enters the Great Marketrealm has a story.

Our purpose is to preserve those stories.

Every portrait.

Every achievement.

Every kingdom explored.

Every honour earned.

Every companion met.

Every legend created.

The Companion exists to ensure none of them are forgotten.

---

# The Guild Archives

The Guild Archives are a real place within the Great Marketrealm.

Players are not interacting with software.

They are visiting the Guild Archives.

Every screen, every action and every system should reinforce this illusion.

Technology should disappear behind the world.

---

# Auby

Auby is not a mascot.

Auby is the Keeper of the Guild Archives.

He is also the Guild's Master Illuminator.

His duties include:

• welcoming new adventurers
• painting Guild Portraits
• maintaining Guild Records
• recording achievements
• protecting forgotten histories
• preserving the stories of every hero

Auby never feels like a tutorial.

He feels like someone who genuinely loves his work.

---

# Portraits

Portraits are not generated.

Portraits are illuminated.

Every portrait is hand-painted by Auby using the Guild Illuminator.

The software merely represents this process.

Portraits should always feel:

• warm
• welcoming
• expressive
• handcrafted
• full of personality

Never mechanical.

Never random.

Never sterile.

---

# Characters

Players do not create database records.

Players create Guild Records.

The Guild Record represents an adventurer's life.

Every decision should reinforce that feeling.

---

# Every Feature Belongs In The World

Before adding any feature we ask:

"How would this exist if the Great Marketrealm were real?"

Examples:

Instead of:

Settings

consider:

Guild Office

Instead of:

Inventory

consider:

Pack & Provisions

Instead of:

Achievements

consider:

Guild Honours

Instead of:

Delete Character

consider:

Remove Guild Record

The fiction always comes first.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Companion should always feel:

gentle

warm

optimistic

welcoming

whimsical

hopeful

illustrated

handcrafted

It should never feel:

clinical

cold

corporate

generic

aggressive

overly technical

The player should feel like they are reading an old storybook.

---

# The Guild Illuminator

The Guild Illuminator is the heart of the Companion.

It is responsible for bringing adventurers to life.

Every portrait follows the same artistic language.

Every race belongs to the same illustrated world.

Every layer exists because Auby painted it.

---

# Artwork

Every illustration should follow the Character Bible.

Characters are built from:

silhouette

construction

expression

lighting

palette

equipment

personality

The goal is consistency.

The goal is warmth.

The goal is charm.

---

# Accessibility

Everyone should be able to visit the Guild Archives.

Accessibility is never an afterthought.

It is part of the Guild's responsibility to welcome every adventurer.

---

# Engineering

Good engineering should disappear.

Players should never think about:

repositories

controllers

renderers

services

containers

Instead they should experience:

Guild Records

Portraits

Kingdoms

Honours

Archives

The code exists to preserve the illusion.

---

# Testing

Every feature should be tested.

Reliability allows creativity.

The stronger the foundations become, the more magical the experience can feel.

---

# Future Contributors

If you contribute to the Great Marketrealm Companion...

You are becoming one of the Guild's Illuminators.

Leave the Guild Archives better than you found them.

Write code with care.

Create artwork with love.

Tell stories with kindness.

Every contribution becomes part of the world's history.

---

# Our Promise

We will never build features for the sake of features.

Every addition should deepen the player's connection to the Great Marketrealm.

Every screen should tell a story.

Every interaction should feel magical.

Every adventurer deserves to be remembered.

---

Signed,

The Guild Illuminators

Keeper of the Guild Archives

Auby
