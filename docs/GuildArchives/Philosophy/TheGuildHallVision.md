# The Guild Hall Vision

## The central promise

The Great Marketrealm Companion is not presented as a collection of software
screens.

It is a place the player returns to.

> **Welcome back to the Guild Hall.**

Every major feature should feel as though it belongs somewhere within that
place and has been quietly cared for between visits.

## Auby's role

Auby is not a generic helper widget.

Auby is the Keeper of the Guild Archives and a working member of the Guild
Hall. His presence should be communicated through:

- his desk;
- illuminated portraits;
- handwritten notes;
- stamps and seals;
- tools left behind;
- small changes in the workspace;
- contextual observations.

The player should often feel that Auby was there just before they arrived.

## The desk

Auby's Desk is the visual anchor of the Guild Hall.

It changes by the visitor's local time of day:

- morning;
- daytime;
- evening;
- night.

These changes are decorative and must never change gameplay or required
information.

## Physical metaphors

Where possible, Companion systems should be expressed through physical Guild
objects:

- Character Sheet -> Guild Journal;
- Inventory -> Leather Satchel;
- Achievements -> Book of Deeds / Guild Honours;
- Campaign history -> Adventurer's Chronicle;
- notifications -> Auby's Notes;
- approval -> Auby Seal of Approval.

## The test

When designing a screen, ask:

> Does this feel like software, or does this feel like a place inside the
> Great Marketrealm?

If it feels like software, look for a Guild Hall metaphor before adding more
interface chrome.
