# The Day the Apple Blinked

**Date:** 7 August 2026

> *Today, the Guild Illuminator smiled back.*

After successive Golden Apple passes, the Apple Fructan Grocer became the
first fully living Generation 2 Guild Portrait.

It could:

- breathe;
- blink using a dedicated painted face overlay;
- sway its leaves;
- carry drifting illuminated pollen;
- receive Auby's final painted flourish.

The milestone concluded with:

```text
1522 tests
2227 assertions
100% passing
```

The event became known within the Guild Archives as:

> **The Day the Apple Blinked.**

From this point onward, living Guild Portraits became part of the visual
language of the Great Marketrealm Companion.
