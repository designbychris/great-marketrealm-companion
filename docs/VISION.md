The Great Marketrealm Companion

The Great Marketrealm Companion exists to make players smile.

It is designed to feel like an artefact from the world itself rather than software outside it.

Every interaction should encourage curiosity, creativity and storytelling.

The Companion should be as enjoyable to explore between sessions as it is during them.

If players forget they are using a web application and instead feel like they are reading a magical Guild Ledger, we have succeeded.
