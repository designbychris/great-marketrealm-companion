# The Twelve Principles of Great Marketrealm Art

1. Round before sharp.
2. Friendly before realistic.
3. Warm before dramatic.
4. Expressive before detailed.
5. Every character tells a story.
6. Equipment should feel well-loved.
7. Nature is always slightly magical.
8. Colours should feel edible.
9. Every silhouette should be recognisable.
10. Light comes from hope, not darkness.
11. Small details reward curiosity.
12. If Auby belongs beside it, it belongs in the Great Marketrealm.
