# Steward's Spell Workshop

The Spell Workshop adds a persistent Steward-authored spell layer beside the protected Players Handbook Spell Register. Draft and archived records remain sealed. Published records enter the shared Spell Register only after mechanical validation.

Published Steward spells receive stable `steward-spell-*` keys, cannot overwrite canonical spell keys, and may enter Character spell catalogues only when level, school, class access, casting time, range, duration and rules text are complete.
