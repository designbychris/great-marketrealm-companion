# Phase III.16.19 — The Steward's Workshop

Phase III.16.19 establishes a persistent custom-content layer for the Great Marketrealm Companion and opens the first authoring surface: the Monster Workshop.

## Three Bestiary sources

1. **Canonical records** remain code-backed and may be curated through the existing canonical override system.
2. **Steward creations** are authored in WordPress, stored separately, and can be Draft, Published, or Archived.
3. **DM Monster Ledger entries** remain private to their Dungeon Master and are not promoted into shared world content.

Published Steward creatures join the shared Bestiary and encounter selector. Draft and archived records remain sealed. Player Field Guide visibility is separately opt-in and uses a dedicated spoiler-safe description.

The custom-content registry is intentionally introduced with monsters first. Spells, Backgrounds, Equipment, Classes and Calling Paths can adopt the same canonical + Steward-authored projection in later phases without changing the protected canonical source files.
